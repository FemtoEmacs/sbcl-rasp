## Q01

The authors used the **Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) 9 registries**. They included adults aged **≥20 years** diagnosed from **1999–2019**. The analysis included **21,948 STS patients** and **3,716 GIST patients**. Patients diagnosed before 1999 were excluded because the modern concept of GIST was not established until the discovery of the **c-KIT gene in 1998**.
